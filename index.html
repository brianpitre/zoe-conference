Guest Speakers

Lisa Harper

Lisa Harper is a compelling communicator whose writing and speaking emphasize that acquiring knowledge about God pales next to an intimate relationship with Jesus. Her style combines sound biblical exposition and exegesis with engaging anecdotes and comedic wit. She’s been featured on numerous television and radio programs, hosts the Lisa Harper’s Back Porch Theology podcast, is a best-selling author and has spoken at hundreds of churches and women’s events. Lisa lives in Nashville with her daughter, Missy.

Manny Arango

Dr. Manny Arango is a Bible nerd and the founder of ARMA Courses, an online educational platform equipping Christians to become biblically literate. He earned a doctorate in New Testament studies from North Baptist Theological Seminary. Manny and his beautiful wife, Tia, are the senior pastors of The Garden, which launched in February 2026 in Houston, TX. They have been married for 12 years and have a son named Theophilus.

Life Women’s Foundation

Kindness that Builds a Life

Life Women’s Foundation was established to show God’s kindness and to honor the journey of women in our world. The power of simple acts of kindness and generosity speaks louder than words and is able to lift the life of someone experiencing a difficult season. As we honor others who are fighting life through with bravery and triumphing through adversity, it builds our own lives.

Each year, we celebrate the lives of some of the heroines in our midst. We surprise them with gifts, lavish them with our love and words of encouragement, cheer them on and witness God working in their lives. Our hope is to bring a piece of God’s goodness to them — to let them feel God’s love and our love too. You’ll have the opportunity to sow into the Life Women’s Foundation through our annual offering and through our Foundation Store, where all proceeds benefit the Foundation.

Good to know

Questions, Answered

What is included in the cost?

Registration includes all sessions, the After Show on Friday, breakfast on Saturday and a special gift. We’ll have fun photo opportunities and you can shop in the Zoe Foundation Store.

What is the After Show?

On Friday night, join us for our first-ever Zoe After Show! Following our evening session, we will have a short intermission and then reconvene in the main auditorium for a hosted event packed with fun, laughter and unforgettable moments. From live entertainment and audience games to incredible prizes and giveaways, there is something for everyone to enjoy.

Can I bring my children with me?

We do not offer childcare during Zoe Conference. All children, including infants, are not permitted in the building. If your daughter is in 6th through 12th grade, she can attend the conference by registering at our special student rate.

What should I bring?

We encourage you to bring a Bible, notebook and a friend! And if you get cold easily, consider a light sweater or jacket for the auditorium.
