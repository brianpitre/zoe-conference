Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before you join us in the garden.

Can I bring my children with me?

We do not offer childcare during Zoe Conference. All children, including infants, are not permitted in the building.

If your daughter is in 6th through 12th grade, she can attend the conference by registering at our special student rate.

What is included in the cost?

Registration includes all sessions, the After Show on Friday, breakfast on Saturday and a special gift. We’ll have fun photo opportunities and you can shop in the Zoe Foundation Store.

What is the After Show?

On Friday night, join us for our first-ever Zoe After Show! Following our evening session, we will have a short intermission and then reconvene in the main auditorium for a hosted event packed with fun, laughter and unforgettable moments. From live entertainment and audience games to incredible prizes and giveaways, there is something for everyone to enjoy.

How soon do I need to register?

We recommend registering as soon as possible to reserve your spot!

Can I buy tickets at the door?

We will sell tickets on the day of the event if we have availability.

Can I just pay for the sessions I attend?

All Zoe Conference tickets are priced to give you access to the entire conference, rather than paying per session. Registration fees are non-refundable.

Are there refunds for tickets?

All ticket sales are final. If you cannot attend, you can give your tickets to a friend or family member.

Will I receive e-tickets for my order?

Yes. As soon as the order transaction is complete, you will receive a confirmation email. Your e-tickets are attached to your confirmation email as a PDF file. You can show your e-tickets on your phone for admission or you can print them. Please check your junk email folder if the confirmation email is not in your inbox.

What do I need for check-in?

Check-in is available at the conference starting Friday at 6:00p and at our main building entrances in the front and back. For a smooth check-in, have your e-ticket ready to scan when you arrive.

Do I need my registration wristband on both days?

Yes! Wristbands are required for entry into the building. If you forget or lose your wristband, just stop by one of our check-in stations.

Is seating assigned?

All seating is general admission, so arrive early to secure a great seat. The building doors will open one hour before all sessions. Be sure to give yourself plenty of time for parking and check-in so you are in your seat when we begin.

What should I bring?

We encourage you to bring a Bible, notebook and a friend!

What should I wear?

If you get cold easily, consider bringing a light sweater or jacket for the auditorium.

Is a Spanish translation of the sessions available?

Translation services are available in Spanish during the conference.

What if I need assistance?

If you need assistance with wheelchair seating or a hearing or visual impairment, we’re here to help! Just let our team know by emailing us at zoe@thelifechurch.com.

Can I sponsor someone to attend Zoe?

Every year, our generosity helps make it possible for other women to be a part of Zoe Conference. You can donate at thelifechurch.com/give by selecting the “Zoe Conference Scholarship” fund.

Are there scholarships available?

Yes. We have created partial scholarships for ladies at The Life Church to attend. To apply for a scholarship, please complete the application at zoeconference.com/scholarship. Once the application is complete, you will be added to a waiting list to receive a scholarship as funds become available.

Are there opportunities to serve?

If you attend The Life Church and have completed Discovery, we would love for you to serve at Zoe Conference.

Other questions?

Connect with our team at zoe@thelifechurch.com. We’d love to help in any way we can!
